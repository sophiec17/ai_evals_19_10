## 1. Product Strategy, The Context

**Who it's for:** VP-level strategists and product leaders at Fortune 500 companies who pay upward of $50,000 a year for verified market intelligence.

**What they use it for:** Asking plain-language questions, like “Compare the pricing models of Stripe and Adyen,” and getting answers in seconds instead of digging through the platform.

**What's at stake:** Engagement is dropping, Q4 retention is at risk, and the CPO wants Ascend IQ live for the top 50 enterprise accounts next month. One poor answer to the wrong client can do lasting damage to the relationship.

## 2. Measurements, The Execution

**User promise.** 
For business users, Ascend IQ promises to reduce the time customers spend digging for information so that customers get to the answers they need quickly and efficiently
For business users, Ascend IQ promises that all information is verified and grounded in factual information and data so that customers know they are getting accurate information
For business users, Ascend IQ promises to minimise bias so that customer make more informed and confident decisions 

**Top 3 trust metrics:**
- **Latency**, Response speed (P95 / P99). Slow kills engagement.
- **Hallucination Rate**, % of outputs that are confidently false or fabricated.
- **Fairness**, Quality consistency across user groups, geos, languages.

**Why these three:** Latency helps measures how fast and responsive our solution is
Hallucination rate helps measures the accuracy and reliability of the responses ensuring information is grounded in data 
Fairness ensures quality is consistency and do not provide bias towards one company or groups of companies

## 3. Strategic Trade-Offs, The Cost

### Trade-off 1 · Fairness ↔ Latency
We prioritise fairness over latency to ensure that generated reports are objective and do not favour specific companies. This enables business users to access balanced insights and know that Ascend IQ is trustworthy platform

### Trade-off 2 · Hallucination Rate ↔ Latency
We prioritise minimising hallucinations over reducing latency to ensure that the marketing intelligence reports generated by Ascend IQ are accurate, verified, and reliable for business users. This builds trust in the insights provided and reduces the time users spend cross-checking information across the platform to validate its accuracy.
